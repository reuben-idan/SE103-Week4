Which phrases were too vague:

How I made each vague part specific:

I used an empty list named failingScores to store only scores that meet the failing rule. I added a decision after the loop to display either the failing scores or a clear message when the list is empty.

Why refinement matters before coding:
Refinement removes assumptions that could lead to the wrong program behavior. It makes the order of decisions and repeated actions clear before writing code. Clear pseudocode is easier to test with examples and explain to another person.
